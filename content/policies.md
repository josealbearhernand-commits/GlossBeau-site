## Shipping Policy

*Last updated: October 4, 2026*

### Where we ship
We currently ship to addresses within the United States, including APO/FPO addresses. We do not ship internationally at this time.

### Processing time
Orders are packed and shipped within 1–3 business days. We do not ship on weekends or U.S. holidays. During sales or busy seasons, processing can take a little longer; if your order will be delayed, we'll email you.

### Shipping options and delivery times

| Method | Cost | Estimated delivery after shipping |
|---|---|---|
| Standard | $8, free on orders over $90 | 5–7 business days |
| Expedited | $15 | 2–3 business days |

Delivery times are estimates from the shipping carrier and are not guaranteed.

### Shipping costs
- **Free standard shipping** on U.S. orders over $90 (after discounts, before taxes).
- On orders under $90, standard shipping is a flat $8.
- Expedited shipping is a flat $15 on any order.

### Tracking your order
When your order ships, you'll receive an email with a tracking number. If you haven't received it within 3 business days of placing your order, contact us at [SUPPORT EMAIL].

### Items that ship separately or have limits
Some products, such as aerosols, nail polish removers, and certain chemical treatments, are regulated as hazardous materials and can only ship by ground. If your order includes one of these items, expedited shipping may not be available for it.

### Lost, late, or damaged packages
- If tracking shows your package was delivered but you can't find it, check with neighbors and your building's mailroom, then contact us within 7 days of the delivery date. We'll open a claim with the carrier and help you resolve it.
- If your package arrives damaged, take photos of the box and the products and email them to [SUPPORT EMAIL] within 7 days of delivery.

### Wrong address
Please double-check your shipping address at checkout. If you notice a mistake, email us right away. We can update the address only if the order hasn't shipped yet. We are not responsible for orders delivered to an incorrect address entered at checkout.

### Questions
Email [SUPPORT EMAIL].

## Return & Refund Policy

*Last updated: October 4, 2026*

We want you to love what you order. Because hair, nail, and skin products are personal care items, we follow these rules to keep every product safe and sealed for every customer.

### What you can return (within 30 days of delivery)
- **Hair care, nail, and barber products** that are **unopened, unused, and still sealed** in their original packaging.
- **Tools and electrical appliances** (dryers, flat irons, curling irons, clippers, trimmers, brushes) that are **unused**, in their original packaging, with all parts, manuals, and protective film included.

### What we can't take back
- Opened, used, or unsealed products, including gel polish, color, developer, treatments, and anything with a broken seal, for hygiene and safety reasons.
- Tools that have been used.
- Items marked **Final Sale** or bought on sale.
- Gift cards.
- Kits or bundles with any piece missing or opened.

### How to start a return
1. Email [SUPPORT EMAIL] within 30 days of delivery with your order number and the items you'd like to return.
2. We'll reply with return instructions and the address to send your package to.
3. Pack the items securely in their original packaging.

Packages sent without first contacting us may not be accepted.

### Return shipping
Return shipping is paid by the customer, unless the return is because of our mistake (wrong item, damaged, or defective). We recommend using a trackable shipping service; we can't be responsible for returns lost in transit.

### Damaged, defective, or wrong items
Please check your order when it arrives. If something is damaged, leaking, defective, or not what you ordered, email [SUPPORT EMAIL] **within 7 days of delivery** with your order number and photos. We'll send a replacement or a full refund, including shipping, at no cost to you.

### Tool warranties
Many professional tools (such as BaBylissPRO, Andis, Wahl, and Turbo Power) come with a manufacturer's warranty. If a tool stops working after the 30-day return window, contact us and we'll help you file the warranty claim with the manufacturer.

### Exchanges
The fastest way to get a different item is to return the one you have and place a new order.

### Refunds
Once we receive and inspect your return, we'll email you to let you know whether it was approved. Approved refunds go back to your original payment method within 10 business days. Your bank or card company may take a few more days to post it. Original shipping charges are not refunded unless the return is because of our mistake.

If it has been more than 15 business days since your return was approved and you haven't seen your refund, contact us at [SUPPORT EMAIL].
