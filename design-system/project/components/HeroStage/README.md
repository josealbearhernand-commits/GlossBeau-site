# HeroStage

The homepage opener: a single 1120px card, 16:10 (4:3 on phones), one product at a time, arrows, dots, and a round accent link to the product.

- Provide `slides` (`{title, image, href}`); on the site each slide also has a clip and plays it before gliding to the next (1.6 s glide, 5 s hold on photo-only slides, paused on hover).
- Photo only, no text on the slide. The wordmark sits under the stage.
- Clips come from the `/cinematic-scene` method: FLUX still, approve, Seedance clip; the still is the poster.
